Stay Hydrated

Water is essential for performance and recovery. Aim for at least 8 glasses (64oz) daily.

  • Drink 16-20oz two hours before training
  • Drink 8oz every 15 minutes during intense exercise
  • Rehydrate with electrolytes after sweaty sessions
